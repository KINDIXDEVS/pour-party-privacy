Pour Party Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 2026 · Developer: Kindix Devs

This Privacy Policy explains what information the Pour Party app ("the app", "we", "us") uses and why. We keep it short because we collect very little. Questions? Email us at kindixdevs@gmail.com.

1. The short version

  • You do not need an account. We never ask for your name, email address, phone number, contact list or location.
  • Your game progress is stored on your own phone.
  • The app shows ads (Google AdMob) and offers optional purchases (Google Play, checked with RevenueCat). These services collect some technical data, described below.
  • We do not sell your personal information.

2. Information stored on your phone

Your levels, coins, stars, badges, settings and reminder choices are saved on your device so the game can remember you. We do not receive this information. If you uninstall the app, it is removed from your phone.

3. Information collected by third-party services

The app uses the following services. They run inside the app and collect data under their own privacy policies.

4. Ads and your choices

  • Where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland), the app shows a consent form before personalised ads are used. You can change your choice at any time in the app's consent settings where available.
  • On Android you can reset your advertising ID or turn off ad personalisation in your phone's Settings > Google > Ads.
  • Premium players do not see ads.

5. Permissions the app may use

  • Internet: to show ads and process purchases.
  • Advertising ID: used by AdMob for advertising.
  • Notifications: only if you turn on reminders. Reminders are created on your phone and no personal data is sent to us to do this.
  • Vibration: for game feedback. You can turn it off in Settings.
  • Billing: to make purchases through Google Play.

6. Sharing

We do not sell personal information and we do not share it with anyone except the service providers listed above, which process data to run ads and purchases. If you use the in-game Share button, the text you choose to send is shared by your phone's share menu with the app you pick.

7. Children

Pour Party is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided information through the app, contact us and we will help remove it with the relevant service providers.

8. Your rights and data deletion

Because we do not keep an account or a server database of your personal data, most information can be deleted by uninstalling the app. Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in Europe, the UK GDPR, or California law), you may have rights to access, correct or delete data held about you by the service providers above. Contact us at the email above and we will help you send the request, or contact the providers directly using the links in section 3.

9. Security

Data sent between the app and the services above is encrypted in transit. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, but we work to protect your information.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will change the effective date at the top of this page when we do.
